Writing step. Write a short cold email from Braden to the business.
- Under 110 words. Plain and friendly. No hype or buzzwords ("leverage", "synergy", "AI-powered solutions").
- Greet a person by name only if the evidence names the owner; otherwise use "Hi there,".
- Open with ONE specific detail from the evidence, so it is clearly not a mass email.
- Pitch only the best_offer chosen in the scoring step, framed as money or time saved.
- One low-friction ask (a 10-minute call, or "want me to show you how it'd work?").
- Never state a fact, number, name or quote that is not in the evidence.
- If best_offer is "none" and the business is open, write a two-line polite note with no pitch.
- If the business is permanently closed, set subject to "N/A" and email_body to "NO_EMAIL".
- Sign off as Braden, and leave [PHONE] as a placeholder.
