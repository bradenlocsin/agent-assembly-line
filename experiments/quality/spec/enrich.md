Research step. From the evidence, determine:
- what_they_do: one plain sentence.
- online_booking: "yes" if the evidence says customers can book or order online (even if only some
  customers can); "no" if it says booking is by phone, text, DM, email, or a contact/request form
  that needs a reply; "unknown" if the evidence is silent or contradicts itself.
- chat_or_text_widget: "yes" or "no" only if the evidence says so; otherwise "unknown".
- review_presence: "strong" = 100+ reviews and latest review within 3 months; "weak" = fewer than
  20 reviews, latest review over 6 months ago, or no reviews; "some" = any other review information;
  "unknown" = no review information at all.
- signals: up to 6 short, concrete facts that matter for automation, each stated in the evidence.
